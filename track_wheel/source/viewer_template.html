EXCAVATOR 60 MM / TRACK WHEEL R5

In-hub gearmotor final drive · recoil-spring idler

Pololu 37D 150:1 inside the sprocket hub · 1.75:1 internal ring stage · C-600-072-1500 recoil spring

How it works

The gearmotor sits inside the sprocket hub, bolted to a stationary housing on the track frame. Its offset output shaft drives a 21-tooth internal ring in the hub cap through a 12-tooth steel pinion. The hub turns on a 61808 bearing under the sprocket and a 6809 near the frame, so chain tension never loads the motor.

The front idler rides on a yoke that slides in a closed channel. A preloaded recoil spring pushes it forward to keep the chain tight. If a stone gets caught, the idler can recoil 4 mm before it reaches a hard stop.
