N20 LEADSCREW ACTUATOR

Two wires in, end stops built in

The unmodified N20 gearmotor with its own M3 leadscrew sits in a square printed body held by screws. A small board in the side slot carries two Hall switches that see the magnet through a 1 mm printed partition: the rod stops by itself at both ends, and reversing the polarity always drives it back out.
