Meshes are the CAD solids from cad_r12.py (STEP in step/). In the cycle modes every body is placed from the MuJoCo trace (10 Hz, linearly interpolated): what you see is the integrated dynamics, including the boom-pair closure. Manual mode uses the closed-form kinematics. Printed parts: PETG, 6 perimeters (2.5 mm) + 10 % gyroid. Soil forces are not modelled: the cycle moves the payload through air.
