# Handoff: upper body R3 (2026-10-30)

**Status:** the R3 upper body is complete and every digital report passes. No physical print, fit or powered test has been done. This replaces the R2 handoff. The R2 history is in git (commit `967404e` and earlier).

## Request

The user asked for the excavator model to be optimised, with these points (paraphrased):
- The battery compartment was never checked against real 18650 dimensions.
- The upper body had a lot of empty space and looked crude.
- The outer shell needed improving.

The shared task-handoff tools (task `d1abdd16-9d15-4d30-a8dd-8ad8225c3078`) and the workspace service tools were not available, so this local note is the handoff. All jobs were finite foreground runs; no servers were started and no commits were made by the agent.

## What changed

- **Source:**
  - New `source/upper_body.py` builds every part above the slew deck, the purchased hardware placement, the fastener table and the analytic cell-fit function.
  - The R3 constants are in `source/design.py`.
  - `build_vehicle.py` imports these parts. The arm, tracks and slew are unchanged.
- **Layout (Cat 320):**
  - Cab front-left: the same R2 cab, mirrored and moved 23 mm forward.
  - Tank housing and stepped toolbox front-right.
  - Full-width engine hood with louvres, valve-cover nose, exhaust stack and a removable battery hatch.
  - Curved counterweight on the 189 mm tail-swing radius (Cat 320: 2,830 mm at 1:15).
  - Deck: 308 mm wide, with a 12 mm perimeter rim.
- **`CAB_SIDE` is fixed at +1.** The right-side housings and the exhaust do not mirror with it, so −1 would put the cab on top of the tank housing.
- **Parts:**
  - Removed: `18_counterweight_tray`, `19_counterweight_lid` and `23_battery_cradle`.
  - New: `18_counterweight`, `19_engine_hood`, `23_equipment_tray`, `29_hood_hatch`, `30_tank_housing`, `31_toolbox_step`, `32_exhaust_stack` and `33_cell_cradle`.
  - The deck, cab parts, seat, console, retainer and fit coupon changed. The coupon gained a Ø19.7 mm cell groove.
- **Battery:**
  - The cradle is sized from the Samsung INR18650-25R, LG INR18650HG2 and Panasonic NCR18650B datasheets. It accepts flat-top cells Ø18.0–18.6 × 64.7–65.3 mm.
  - The cells form a welded side-by-side 2S1P pack, at most 38.2 × 19.1 × 67.6 mm.
  - Two Ø19.7 mm grooves on a 19.3 mm pitch, with 69 mm between the end walls. The foam pads are 4 mm at the ends and 3 mm on top.
  - The pack lifts out after removing the 4 hatch screws and 2 retainer screws. The BMS and converters stay on the tray.
  - The NCR18650B appears only for dimensions; it is not a 15 A cell. A welded pack was chosen over spring holders because of the 7 A peak draw.
- **Fasteners:** every shell bolts into M3/M4 nuts in deck-underside pockets. All 34 upper-body screws engage the full nut height, and no tip goes below Z124, so the slew stop posts stay clear.
- **Ballast:** a steel plate, 54 × 150 × 24 mm (1.53 kg), clamped inside the counterweight.
  - Calculated mass rose from 11.29 to 11.89 kg.
  - Worst-case stability margin improved from 33.1 to 43.2 mm.
- **Docs:** README/BOM/PDFs are generated by `source/build_docs.py`. `ASSEMBLY.md`, `references/CAB_PROPORTIONS.md`, `references/TRACK_SYSTEM.md` and the root README were edited by hand. `images/r2_r3_comparison.png` shows before and after; `images/r1_r2_comparison.png` is kept as history.

## Open points

- **Large parts:** the deck (327 × 308 mm), hood and counterweight exceed common printer beds. Printer-bed splitting is still deferred, as before.
- **Print supports:** the hood nose and the toolbox step need support when the shells print inverted.
- **Physical checks still needed:**
  - real cells in the coupon groove;
  - foam pad compression;
  - thread engagement of the 80 mm countersunk screws;
  - board hole patterns and cooling;
  - the harness route through the tray slot and the Ø8 deck opening at (-10,25).
