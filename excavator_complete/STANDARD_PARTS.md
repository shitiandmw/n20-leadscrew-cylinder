Every place that takes a bought standard component: bearings and the slewing ring, the slip ring, motors, magnets, heat-set inserts, springs, pins, the battery pack and the connectors, and the fasteners. Positions are centres in the vehicle frame at swing 0 (mm; X forward, Y left, Z up). The same markers are drawn in `3D_preview.html` ("Standard parts" in the layer list: click a marker to read it). Generated by `source/build_connectors.py` from the subsystem scenes and the J9 configuration; the purchase specifications are in [BOM.md](BOM.md) and the connector models in [CONNECTORS.md](CONNECTORS.md).
