# Retained drive fabrication interfaces

These interfaces are unchanged in J8. Use the current J8 STEP assembly, ASSEMBLY.md and BOM.md for the motor plate, housing, frame, rollers and fastening.

- **J01 aluminum carrier:** Ø45 seat for 6809, Ø40 seat for 61808; Ø32 internal bore accessible from its rear; Ø13 shaft clearance through the end wall. Four M3 blind rear holes on R19.25 at cardinal angles; four M3 retainer holes on R15. Nominal geometry uses Ø3 smooth holes to represent threads; drill/tap properly, do not simply drill Ø3. Finish bearing seats to a suitable measured sliding/locating fit and maintain their coaxiality.

- **J04 retainer:** Ø42.8 × 3 mm, Ø13 center clearance, four Ø3.4 holes on R15. Clamps only the 61808 inner ring. Its screw head envelope ends at Y8.15, below the cap boss starting at Y10.

- **J10 steel spindle:** 59.5 mm overall, Ø12 neck; AF24 hex × 6 mm at the outboard end. Blind Ø6.04 D socket, 5.54 mm across diameter-to-flat, 11.1 mm deep, opening at Y−41.5. Sinker EDM or suitable broaching/milling process with controlled relief is needed for the blind D form. Ø3.4 through hole continues from socket bottom to the hex end. M3 × 55 screw seats at Y18; its nominal thread engagement in the motor shaft is 6.6 mm. Socket shoulder seats against shaft end at Y−30.4. Confirm real shaft end/chamfer before machining that shoulder.

- **J03 printed cap:** tough PETG or PA, closed outer face on bed; solid walls around the hex socket and six webs. Deburr and gauge the AF24.2 socket. Nominal clearance is 0.1 mm per opposing face; tune to the printer/material. The spindle hex ends at Y18 and the socket floor at Y19. Do not shim that axial float closed.

Inspect actual motor hole depths, bearing fits and spindle runout before powered testing. The motor mounting screws assume 3 mm thread engagement; the output-shaft depth-10 annotation is a separate feature.
