## Evidence limits

The research used descriptions, photographs, official part previews and file lists, without obtaining exact third-party geometry. Previews do not establish motor identity, capacity, fits or fastener specifications. The retained docking beam and interfaces were independently dimensioned from the JGY-370/R5 CAD. The proposed central opening reserves space only; it does not reproduce the author's slew ring or define a slew bearing.

J8's operation video uses this project's geometry and a saved unloaded motion trace. It is not the author's footage, a physical demonstration or a motor performance test.
