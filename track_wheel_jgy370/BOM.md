The five J621 stepped axles, ten M4 x 8 axle screws, ten associated clamp washers and five 1 mm outer roller spacers are removed. Do not reuse earlier roller shaft collars or long inboard spacers. Idler hardware remains unchanged.

J402 motor plate, J313 docking beam, independent drive bearings and coupling, sprocket, sprung idler, tension adjuster and chain are retained. Their hardware is modeled in the complete assembly. See `references/drive_interfaces.md` for the retained carrier, retainer, spindle and cap fabrication notes, and the sibling `track_wheel/BOM.md` for unchanged chain/idler hardware. J8 specifications take precedence for all replaced parts. The paired reference includes the proposed J314 X connector and its retained four M4 screws.

Fastener heads and threads are simplified nominal envelopes. Match purchased screws, thread depths and tightening requirements before machining the final countersinks and blind threads.
