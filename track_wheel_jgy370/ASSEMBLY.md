# J8 continuous side-frame assembly and service

## Prepare the bracket

Machine the continuous J815 and J816 side plates from 4 mm aluminum. Retain the drive-clearance cutaway on J815, the drive-access hole and 1 mm rear relief on J816, and the idler travel slot in both plates. Establish common X/Z datums and machine/ream their five axle bores as a matched pair. Nominal 8.05 mm holes are a CAD starting point, not a tolerance specification. Select final close sliding fits for the actual ground 8 mm shafts. Keep the plate faces flat and parallel; deburr both ends of every bore.

Machine five J721 steel axles with their integral rear heads, upward flats and 4 mm rear hex sockets. Finish the bearing journals and M5 front blind threads. The front countersink mouth clears the screw cone; the shaft ends at Y26.5, 1.5 mm short of the outer cheek face. The inner cheek supports 4 mm of shaft; the outer cheek supports 2.5 mm before its countersink. Add compatible edge relief and machining allowances without reducing those engagements.

Finish the three 48 mm ties and ten 4 mm end spacers square and parallel. The retained 26 mm spacers must match the actual distance between bearing inner rings. Verify every assembled bearing stack against the tie length; adjust by controlled machining or selected shims, not by forcing the plates together. Ensure that any shims preserve the overall envelope and roller side clearances.

Print J806 and J711 in PA12 using suitable supports and finish their seating faces. J806 includes access pockets for the axle heads and three bracket fasteners; the metal cheeks locate the journals. Do not scale the whole print to correct a local fit. The housing remains approximately 292.65 mm long, with 2.5 mm nominal walls, locally 1 mm over the motor. Its nominal can clearance remains 0.8 mm.

## Assemble the five wheels between the plates

1. Seat J815 against the frame at Y=-28. Fit the three cross ties against its outboard face at Y=-24. From inboard, fit the OD9 washers and M4 x 25 low-head screws through the frame and inner cheek into the ties. Nominal tie engagement is 6 mm. Establish torque without crushing the printed frame.
2. Insert each axle from inboard through its D-shaped access pocket and inner cheek. Keep the head flat upward. The integral head seats against the metal inner cheek, at Y=-28; it does not clamp against the printed pocket.
3. Fit one 4 mm end spacer, then the roller with two 608 bearings and its 26 mm inner-ring spacer, then the other 4 mm end spacer. The stack is 4 + 7 + 26 + 7 + 4 = 48 mm. Spacers bear on the bearing inner rings, clear of seals and the printed wheel body.
4. Slide J816 over all five axle ends. The inner face seats against the three ties and all five metal bearing stacks at Y24. Fit three M4 x 10 countersunk tie screws and five M5 x 12 countersunk axle screws. Counterhold the rear axle sockets as needed. Tighten progressively while checking free rotation; do not use screw force to correct misaligned bores.
5. Verify that screw heads are flush at Y28, all five wheels turn freely, and each flange retains nominal 1 mm clearance to its cheek. Use an appropriate steel-thread locking method after fit and torque are established.

The broad continuous aluminum cheeks and steel ties form the double-sided support for all five small-wheel axles. The large drive wheel retains its own bearing carrier; the idler remains on its sliding yoke. Keep the idler travel openings free. The outer cheek must be fitted for operation. The protective housing does not replace either structural plate.

## Complete the track

Install the retained motor, independently supported drive hub, idler and tension adjuster. Preserve the original motor holes and verify purchased motor thread depths and wire routing. Fit the chain and set tension using the existing adjuster. Install the docking beam at the retained chassis pads. The X connector and mirrored left module remain reference proposals pending actual chassis and left-motor geometry.

Fit J711 using its seven M3 x 10 screws. Its motor shroud and right-hand guard walls are one body. Confirm the actual motor can, wires, print clearances and operating temperature.

## Service sequence

1. Remove the seven housing screws and withdraw the entire housing inboard (-Y) when rear fastener access is needed.
2. Remove all five outer M5 axle screws and all three outer M4 tie screws. Slide the outer cheek outboard (+Y); no chassis or motor removal is required in the nominal model.
3. To remove a wheel, slacken, open and remove the chain first. Slide its outer spacer, wheel/bearings and loose inner spacer off the axle toward +Y. Do not force a roller flange sideways through the installed chain.
4. If removing the axle, remove both 4 mm end spacers and the wheel first, then withdraw the axle inboard (-Y). Keep its head flat upward. The motor and docking beam remain installed in the checked sample positions.
5. Reassemble both cheeks before operation. Recheck wheel freedom, side clearance, fastener retention and track alignment.

The checks cover nominal solids and sampled positions. They do not establish payload capacity, fatigue, fastener preload, printed-frame creep or continuous collision-free sweeps. Validate actual fits, bracket stiffness and retention under progressively applied loads before evaluating endurance.
